# Motion DNA

Motion should be treated as brand behavior when it recurs consistently.

## Motion character

Analyze:

- speed
- restraint
- elasticity
- precision
- inertia
- smoothness
- abruptness
- playfulness
- cinematic quality
- mechanical quality

## Timing

When measurable:

- duration
- delay
- stagger

Otherwise use perceptual categories.

## Easing

Use perceptual descriptions unless exact curves are measured.

Examples:

- linear
- soft
- decisive ease-out
- spring-like
- elastic
- inertial

## Motion hierarchy

Identify priority:

- brand/logo
- hero
- media
- typography
- controls
- microinteraction
- ambient motion

## Motion types

- entrance
- exit
- hover
- click
- press
- loading
- scroll
- media
- route transition
- ambient
- direct manipulation

## Relationship to personality

Connect motion to brand personality.

Example:

A precise premium brand may consistently favor slow controlled transitions
with minimal overshoot.

## Channel status

Mark each motion behavior as:

- core brand behavior
- web-specific
- campaign-specific
- execution-specific
- unknown
