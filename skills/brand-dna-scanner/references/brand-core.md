# Brand Core Analysis

Analyze when evidence allows:

## Strategic core

- purpose
- mission
- vision
- promise
- category
- positioning
- audience
- differentiation
- reasons to believe
- functional benefits
- emotional benefits
- symbolic benefits

## Cultural core

- cultural position
- worldview
- values
- principles
- beliefs
- boundaries
- aspirations
- tensions

## Personality

Measure:

- authority
- warmth
- sophistication
- irreverence
- precision
- experimentation
- optimism
- seriousness
- sensuality
- intellectuality
- playfulness
- restraint
- confidence

## Archetypes

Use archetypes only when supported.

Do not force every brand into one archetype.

Multiple or contradictory archetypal signals may coexist.

## Declared vs observed

Always distinguish:

DECLARED:
what the brand says it is

OBSERVED:
how it consistently behaves

GAP:
where the two differ
