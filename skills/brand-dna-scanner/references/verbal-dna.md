# Verbal DNA

Analyze language as a system.

## Voice

- formal / informal
- distant / intimate
- rational / emotional
- technical / accessible
- authoritative / conversational
- serious / humorous
- literal / metaphorical
- restrained / expressive

## Syntax

Inspect:

- sentence length
- fragments
- punctuation
- rhythm
- parallelism
- questions
- imperative constructions
- first/second/third person
- active/passive voice

## Vocabulary

Identify:

- recurring words
- proprietary terminology
- product vocabulary
- category vocabulary
- cultural language
- technical language
- banned/off-brand language when inferable

## Messaging structures

Inspect:

- taglines
- claims
- headlines
- subheads
- CTAs
- body copy
- product descriptions
- navigation
- forms
- errors
- confirmations
- social captions

## Storytelling

Identify whether narratives tend to be:

- product-first
- customer-first
- founder-first
- idea-first
- transformation-first
- evidence-first

## Evidence balance

Measure use of:

- emotion
- proof
- statistics
- testimonials
- authority
- process
- features
- outcomes

## Naming

Analyze:

- product naming
- campaign naming
- feature naming
- capitalization
- invented words
- descriptive vs evocative names

## Localization

Inspect whether the brand uses:

- literal translation
- transcreation
- localized naming
- region-specific tone
