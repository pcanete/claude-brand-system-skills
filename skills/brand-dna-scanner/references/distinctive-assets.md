# Distinctive Brand Assets

The goal is to identify recognition-driving assets.

Possible assets:

- logo
- symbol
- color
- type
- shape
- pattern
- photography
- illustration
- phrase
- naming
- motion
- sound
- product silhouette
- packaging
- interaction pattern
- recurring composition

## Asset scoring

Each candidate asset should support:

### Salience

How noticeable is it?

### Recurrence

How often is it used?

### Consistency

How consistently?

### Distinctiveness

How uncommon is it?

### Ownership confidence

How likely is it to be associated specifically with this brand rather than
the category?

## Minimum recognition set

Determine the smallest combination likely to preserve recognizable identity.

Examples:

- type + color + photographic crop
- shape + verbal rhythm + motion
- product silhouette + material + typography

## Category-code warning

A common category convention may have:

high recurrence

but:

low distinctiveness

Do not call it proprietary.
