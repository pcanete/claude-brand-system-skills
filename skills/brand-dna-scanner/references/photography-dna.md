# Photography DNA

Analyze photographic language across multiple examples.

## Subject

- people
- product
- architecture
- landscape
- detail
- process
- lifestyle
- abstract

## Casting

When relevant:

- styling
- pose
- expression
- gaze
- body language
- movement
- relationship to camera

Do not infer protected/sensitive traits.

## Camera language

Analyze:

- approximate lens character
- perspective
- distance
- depth
- depth of field
- camera height
- angle
- crop
- framing
- symmetry/asymmetry

Do not fabricate exact focal lengths without evidence.

## Lighting

Analyze:

- source
- direction
- softness
- contrast
- highlight behavior
- shadow character
- color temperature
- flash character
- natural/studio feel

## Environment

Analyze:

- location
- set
- background
- props
- material context
- environmental texture

## Post-production

Analyze:

- contrast
- saturation
- black level
- highlight rolloff
- color grading
- grain
- sharpness
- skin treatment
- film/digital character
- retouching style

## Recurring motifs

Identify visual motifs recurring across independent campaigns or product
families.

## Don'ts

Infer what would feel off-brand only from sufficiently strong positive
evidence.

Example:

If almost all imagery uses controlled sculptural lighting, generic bright
stock-office photography may be marked incompatible.
