# Optional Channels

Channels that only some brands express, and that most scans have no material
for. Analyze one only when evidence for it actually exists. An empty section is
a correct result; an invented one contaminates the whole model.

Whatever you conclude here follows the same rule as everything else: it belongs
in `observations` with its evidence, and coverage for the channel stays at
whatever the material earned.

## Product, packaging and physical

When evidence exists analyze:

- form
- silhouette
- materials
- proportions
- labels
- typography
- tactile cues
- unboxing
- premium cues
- sustainability cues
- retail presentation
- relationship between physical and digital identity

The relationship between physical and digital is often the most revealing part:
a brand whose packaging and interface disagree is telling you which one is
current.

## Environmental identity

When evidence exists analyze:

- architecture and interiors
- signage
- wayfinding
- spatial typography
- material language
- lighting
- color
- exhibition and event design
- object placement
- sensory cues

Retail and event photography is usually third-party. Record authority
accordingly: a press photo of a store is evidence of the store, not of the
brand's own art direction.

## Sonic identity

When evidence exists analyze:

- sonic logo
- voice
- music
- tempo
- instrumentation
- interaction sounds
- ambience
- silence
- recurring sonic motifs

Music in a single campaign film is campaign expression until it recurs. Silence
is a deliberate choice in some identities; record its absence as such rather
than leaving the section empty without comment.

## Brand behavior

Analyze:

- customer-service tone
- onboarding
- selling style
- education
- exclusivity
- scarcity
- transparency
- community
- social responses
- error recovery
- cultural participation

Behavior is the hardest channel to observe first-hand and the easiest to
project onto a brand. Only infer it when enough evidence exists, and prefer
observed interactions — replies, onboarding flows, error states — over
statements the brand makes about itself.
