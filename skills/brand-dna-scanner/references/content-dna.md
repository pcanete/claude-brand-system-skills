# Content DNA

Analyze what the brand repeatedly chooses to talk about and how.

## Categories

Possible categories:

- product
- culture
- education
- process
- case studies
- customers
- founder
- research
- proof
- impact
- news
- behind the scenes
- community
- campaigns

## Content balance

Estimate relationship between:

- narrative vs data
- emotional vs rational
- product vs culture
- sales vs education
- short vs long-form
- brand vs founder
- visual vs text

## Proof mechanisms

Analyze use of:

- metrics
- testimonials
- awards
- client logos
- case studies
- press
- demonstrations
- scientific/technical evidence

## Narrative structure

Identify recurring patterns such as:

problem
→ process
→ result

or:

idea
→ cultural context
→ product

## CTA system

Analyze:

- frequency
- language
- urgency
- softness
- directness
- placement
- repetition

## Content modules

Identify reusable content families.
